The Person Behind the Mission

A journey rooted in compassion, purpose and service.

Dr. Farmaan Hasan Khan is a humanitarian, social activist and advocate for peace whose work centres on education, healthcare, social welfare and community harmony.

Born in Bareilly, Uttar Pradesh, he has sought to connect the values of faith and compassion with practical initiatives for people in need. His work includes educational assistance, medical support, youth development and efforts to promote mutual understanding.

As the founder of Ala Hazrat Tajushariah Welfare Society and National General Secretary of Jama'at Raza-e-Mustafa, he remains engaged in initiatives that seek to create opportunities and strengthen communities.

Institutional Commitment

Ala Hazrat Tajushariah Welfare Society

Founded by Dr. Farmaan Hasan Khan, the Ala Hazrat Tajushariah Welfare Society is dedicated to humanitarian assistance, educational opportunity, healthcare and community welfare.

Through its programmes, the organisation seeks to support vulnerable communities and encourage a culture of compassion, responsibility and collective service.
