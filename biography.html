01

Early Life & Education

Roots, Heritage & Academic Foundation

Dr. Farmaan Hasan Khan (affectionately known as Farman Mian) was born on 8 August 1995 in Bareilly, Uttar Pradesh—a historically significant hub of Islamic scholarship and spiritual legacy in North India.

Coming from a prestigious family closely associated with the intellectual and spiritual heritage of Aala Hazrat Imam Ahmed Raza Khan Barelvi, he developed a profound sense of civic responsibility and social service from an early age.

He pursued higher education at Mahatma Jyotiba Phule Rohilkhand University (MJPRU), completing his Bachelor of Arts and Bachelor of Laws (BA LL.B). Equipped with legal acumen, he enrolled as an advocate practicing at the Allahabad High Court, utilizing law as a tool for public advocacy and legal empowerment for the underprivileged.

02

Leadership & Governance

Jama'at Raza-e-Mustafa & Welfare Society

National General Secretary — Jama'at Raza-e-Mustafa

In 2021, Dr. Farmaan Hasan Khan assumed the role of National General Secretary of Jama'at Raza-e-Mustafa, an iconic socio-religious organization established in 1920 by Imam Ahmed Raza Khan Barelvi. Under his leadership, the organization expanded its footprint into community development, relief work, and interfaith dialogue across India.

Founder — Aala Hazrat Tajushariah Welfare Society

To institutionalize humanitarian service, he founded the Aala Hazrat Tajushariah Welfare Society. The non-profit operates internationally with core focus areas in free medical surgeries, competitive exam coaching (NEET/JEE), tree plantation drives, and women empowerment initiatives.
