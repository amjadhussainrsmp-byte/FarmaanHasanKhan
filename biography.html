A JOURNEY OF SERVICE AND PURPOSE
An Introduction

A journey rooted in faith, service and humanity.

Waqaar-e-Millat Hazrat Dr. Farmaan Hasan Khan, popularly known as Farmaan Mian, is an Indian humanitarian, social activist, community worker and advocate for peace. Born in Bareilly, Uttar Pradesh, he grew up in an environment deeply connected with faith, compassion and public service.

His work reflects a commitment to the welfare of society, particularly the upliftment of underprivileged communities, the advancement of education, access to healthcare and the promotion of social harmony.

Through organisational leadership and grassroots initiatives, he seeks to translate the values of compassion and service into meaningful action.

“
True leadership begins with service and ends with unity.
Farmaan Hasan Khan
His Background

Early Life & Education

The foundations of a life dedicated to public service were shaped by family values, education and a strong sense of social responsibility.

Early Life

Born on 8 August 1995 in Bareilly, India, Farmaan Hasan Khan grew up in a family closely connected with the city's spiritual and cultural heritage. From an early age, he was inspired by the values of compassion, truth, dignity and selfless service.

These formative influences contributed to his interest in social welfare, community development, justice and peaceful coexistence.

Education

He pursued his higher education in law and completed his B.A. LL.B. from Mahatma Jyotiba Phule Rohilkhand University, Bareilly.

His educational background, combined with his engagement in community affairs, has informed his approach to social responsibility and public service.

Values That Guide His Work

His approach to public service is guided by the belief that compassion should lead to action and that the dignity of every individual deserves respect.


He advocates education, healthcare access, community participation, women's empowerment and peaceful relations among people of different backgrounds.

Public Engagement

Roles & Contributions

His work brings together organisational responsibility, community engagement and humanitarian service.

01

Founder — Ala Hazrat Tajushariah Welfare Society

Farmaan Hasan Khan founded the Ala Hazrat Tajushariah Welfare Society, an organisation committed to humanitarian assistance, education, healthcare, skill development and social welfare. Through its initiatives, the society works to support marginalised and underserved communities.

02

National General Secretary — Jama'at Raza-e-Mustafa

He also serves as the National General Secretary of Jama'at Raza-e-Mustafa, a historic organisation associated with the teachings and legacy of Aala Hazrat Imam Ahmed Raza Khan Faazil-e-Bareilly. His responsibilities include organisational engagement and initiatives related to community welfare, awareness and harmony.

Service to Society

Humanitarian Work & Initiatives

His humanitarian engagement focuses on practical support and opportunities for people who need them most.

Through the Ala Hazrat Tajushariah Welfare Society, he has been associated with programmes designed to address educational, medical and social needs.

These initiatives include medical assistance, support for surgeries, educational opportunities, skill training and community welfare activities. Particular attention is directed towards underprivileged families, women and young people.

During important community gatherings and occasions associated with Dargah-e-Aala Hazrat, welfare activities provide an opportunity to connect the spirit of service with practical assistance.

Service Beyond Words

For Farmaan Hasan Khan, humanitarian work is not merely an organisational activity. It is an expression of responsibility towards society and a commitment to helping people with dignity.

A Shared Society

Peace, Brotherhood & Social Harmony

Alongside humanitarian activities, Farmaan Hasan Khan emphasises the importance of peace, mutual respect and understanding among communities.

His public engagement reflects an interest in bringing people together through dialogue, social awareness and collective responsibility.

He believes that a healthy society is built not only through institutions but also through empathy, cooperation and respect for human dignity.
