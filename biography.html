An Introduction

A journey rooted in faith, compassion and purpose.

Farmaan Hasan Khan, popularly known as Farmaan Mian, is an Indian, religious leader, social activist, humanitarian and advocate for peace. Born in Bareilly, India, he grew up in a family deeply rooted in faith, compassion and public service.

As the National General Secretary of Jama'at Raza-e-Mustafa, a historic organisation associated with the teachings of Aala Hazrat Imam Ahmed Raza Khan Faazil-e-Bareilly, he is engaged in organisational and community work.

Through the Ala Hazrat Tajushariah Welfare Society, which he founded in 2019, he has worked towards humanitarian assistance, education, healthcare and community welfare.

His Beginnings

Early Life & Education

The formative years, educational journey and values that shaped his engagement with public life.

Born on 8 August 1995 in Bareilly, Uttar Pradesh, Farmaan Hasan Khan grew up in a family closely connected with the city's spiritual heritage. From an early age, he was inspired by the values of compassion, truth and selfless service.

These formative influences nurtured his interest in social reform, justice, spiritual harmony and the welfare of people. His journey reflects an effort to connect the values of faith with practical community service.

He completed his B.A. LL.B. from Mahatma Jyotiba Phule Rohilkhand University, Bareilly. His educational background and engagement with community affairs have informed his approach to social responsibility.

After completing his legal education, he stepped into public life with a focus on helping people at the grassroots level and contributing to the welfare of underserved communities.

Family & Heritage

Rooted in a tradition of faith and service.

Farmaan Hasan Khan's family background is closely connected with Bareilly's religious and spiritual heritage. His life and public work are situated within a tradition that places importance on faith, community responsibility and service.

He is the son-in-law of Shahzada-e-Huzoor Tajushshariah, Qazi-ul-Quzzat Fil Hind, Huzoor Qaid-e-Millat, Hazrat Allamah Maulana Mufti Muhammad Asjad Raza Khan Qadri Razvi, known as the Grand Mufti of India and Islamic Chief Justice of India. His public identity and work, however, are also reflected through his own organisational and humanitarian engagement.

Public & Organisational Life

Roles & Contributions

His organisational responsibilities connect humanitarian work, community welfare and public engagement.

FOUNDER

Ala Hazrat Tajushariah Welfare Society

Farmaan Hasan Khan is the founder of the Ala Hazrat Tajushariah Welfare Society, an organisation dedicated to humanitarian aid, education, healthcare and the promotion of communal harmony.

Under his guidance, the society undertakes welfare initiatives aimed at improving the lives of marginalised and underserved communities.

GENERAL SECRETARY

Jama'at Raza-e-Mustafa

He also serves as the General Secretary of Jama'at Raza-e-Mustafa, a historic Islamic organisation associated with the spiritual legacy and teachings of Aala Hazrat Imam Ahmed Raza Khan Faazil-e-Bareilly. The organisation works under the Presidentship of Allamah Maulana Mufti Muhammad Asjad Raza Khan Qadri Razvi, the son and successor of Huzoor Tajush Shariah Hazrat Allamah Maulana Mufti Muhammad Akhtar Raza Khan Qadri Azhari bin Maulana Ibrahim Raza Khan Jilani Miyan.

In this role, he contributes to organisational activities, interfaith dialogue, religious awareness, youth engagement and initiatives promoting peace and social harmony.
