Meet Farmaan Hasan Khan

Rooted in values.
Focused on people.

Known for his involvement in humanitarian work and community service, Farmaan Hasan Khan has built his public mission around practical support for people, with particular attention to healthcare, education, social welfare and harmony.

He is the founder of the Ala Hazrat Tajushariah Welfare Society and serves as National General Secretary of Jama’at Raza-e-Mustafa. His work reflects a commitment to community responsibility and the belief that compassion should translate into meaningful action.

This website offers an introduction to his journey, the initiatives he supports and the people-centred values that guide his work.
