Environmental Conservation

Tree Plantation Drive

Building a greener tomorrow through environmental awareness, community participation and the simple yet meaningful act of planting trees.

A Responsibility Beyond Today

For Farmaan Hasan Khan, social service is not only about helping people in the present. It is also about thinking of the people who will come after us. A cleaner neighbourhood, a healthier environment and a greener tomorrow are all part of the same responsibility.

This belief has shaped his growing work in environmental conservation, particularly through tree plantation drives. What may appear to be a simple act of putting a sapling into the soil carries a much deeper message: if we care about our communities, we must also care about the environment in which those communities live.

Tree plantation is a meaningful way to encourage people to participate in the protection of their surroundings. It brings attention to the importance of greenery and reminds communities that environmental responsibility is something everyone can contribute to.

A Campaign with a Message

Ek Paudha Ala Hazrat Ke Naam

Remembering a respected personality through an act that continues to benefit people, nature and future generations.

The “Ek Paudha Aala Hazrat Ke Naam” Campaign

A significant step in this journey came during the 108th Urs-e-Razvi, with the launch of the “Ek Paudha Aala Hazrat Ke Naam” campaign.

As reported by Live Hindustan, the campaign was launched to promote environmental consciousness and encourage people to plant trees in the name of Aala Hazrat Imam Ahmad Raza Khan Fazil-e-Bareilvi.

The appeal was not restricted to one city or one organisation. Devotees, ulema, imams, madrasas, mosques and social organisations were encouraged to participate and take the message of plantation to their own areas.

The idea behind the campaign is beautifully simple: remember a great personality by planting something that will continue to benefit people and nature.

A tree does not ask who planted it or where a person comes from. It gives shade to everyone. The spirit of environmental service

Planting Trees, Nurturing Communities

A tree is more than a part of the landscape. It contributes to cleaner air, supports biodiversity and quietly becomes part of the life of a community. Its shade can serve people across generations, without distinction.

That makes a tree a particularly meaningful symbol of service. The act of planting is simple, but its benefits can extend far beyond the person who plants it. It is a contribution to shared spaces and to the well-being of people who may never know who planted the tree.

Through this initiative, the emphasis is on encouraging environmental awareness and making plantation a shared community responsibility.

Why Tree Plantation Matters

Environmental conservation begins with awareness, but it becomes meaningful when awareness leads to action. Tree plantation offers individuals and institutions a practical opportunity to participate in creating healthier surroundings.

A Shared Responsibility for the Future

The message of the campaign goes beyond the act of planting a sapling. It encourages people to see environmental care as an essential part of social responsibility.

Whether through an individual effort, a religious institution, an educational centre or a social organisation, every contribution can help strengthen awareness and inspire others to take part.

For Farmaan Hasan Khan, the larger purpose is to encourage a culture in which service to humanity also includes care for the natural world. A greener environment is not merely an aspiration for today; it is a responsibility towards those who will inherit tomorrow.
