On-Ground Programs

6 Core Welfare Pillars

01. Relief Work

Humanitarian Aid Drives

Distributing ration kits, clothing, blankets, and daily essentials to underprivileged families across rural and urban slums during festivals and harsh winters.

02. Peace & Unity

Communal Harmony Campaigns

Organizing interfaith dialogue seminars, brotherhood forums, and peace marches across states to foster social cohesion between communities.

03. Healthcare

Free Health Camps & Surgeries

Conducting free medical checkups, distributing vital medicines, and sponsoring critical surgical operations during annual health relief drives.

04. Skill Building

Women Empowerment

Providing vocational training, sewing machines, and computer skills to help women gain financial independence and dignity.

05. Environment

Ek Paudha Aala Hazrat Ke Naam

Mass tree plantation drives and eco-awareness campaigns launched during Urs-e-Razvi to promote environmental protection nationwide.

06. Youth Awakening

Youth & Character Building

Workshops focused on moral education, discipline, digital skills, and civic responsibility to guide young minds toward positive leadership.
